## 1. Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry

| Field | Topic Order | Free Resources |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mathematics | Arithmetic, fractions, decimals, percentages, algebra, geometry, calculus | [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math) · [OpenStax Mathematics](https://openstax.org/subjects/math) |
| Physics | Measurement, units, motion, force, energy, electricity, waves | [OpenStax Science](https://openstax.org/subjects/science) |
| Chemistry | Matter, atoms, elements, molecules, periodic table, bonding, chemical reactions | [OpenStax Science](https://openstax.org/subjects/science) |

*Sources:* [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org) and [OpenStax](https://openstax.org). OpenStax digital books are free, while printed editions are paid.
