# Automotive, Aerospace & Safety-Critical Systems

Primary standards and official references for automotive/aerospace systems and safety-critical engineering (many standards are paid).

- [NASA Systems Engineering Handbook](https://www.nasa.gov/reference/systems-engineering-handbook/)
- [DDS (OMG Specification)](https://www.omg.org/spec/DDS/)
- [AUTOSAR](https://www.autosar.org/)
- [SAE Standards](https://www.sae.org/standards)
- [RTCA](https://www.rtca.org/)
- [NASA Software Engineering Handbook](https://swehb.nasa.gov/)
