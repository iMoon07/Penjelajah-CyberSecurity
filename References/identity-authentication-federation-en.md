# Identity, Authentication & Federation

Primary standards/specifications and official documentation for digital identity, authentication, federation, and workload identity.

- [OpenID Foundation Specifications](https://openid.net/developers/specs/)
- [OAuth Working Group (IETF Datatracker)](https://datatracker.ietf.org/wg/oauth/documents/)
- [SPIFFE/SPIRE Documentation](https://spiffe.io/docs/)
- [FIDO Alliance Specifications](https://fidoalliance.org/specifications/)
- [JSON Web Token (RFC 7519)](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7519)
- [SCIM (RFC 7644)](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7644)
- [LDAP (RFC 4511)](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4511)
