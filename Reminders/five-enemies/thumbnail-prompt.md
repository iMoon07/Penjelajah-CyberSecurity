# Thumbnail Prompt — Five Enemies (Japanese Fighter / War Theme)

## Prompt (copy-paste, English)

```
A cinematic Japanese-themed book thumbnail, 16:9, 1920x1080.

Subject: a lone wandering swordsman (ronin) in a worn dark kimono and hakama,
standing on a windswept ridge at dusk, seen from behind at a slight low angle.
His katana is sheathed; one hand rests on the hilt. A tattered crimson scarf
snaps in the wind.

Behind and around him rise FIVE towering shadow-warriors (oni-like silhouettes
with glowing eyes), each representing one enemy: Comfort (a soft, seated figure
wrapped in warm cloth), Temper (a red, flame-shrouded warrior), Fear (a huge
jittering black shape), Ego (a figure wearing a cracked golden mask), Time (a
tall thin figure holding an hourglass). They loom but do not attack — they wait.

Style: ukiyo-e inspired woodblock texture mixed with modern cinematic
illustration; heavy ink brush strokes; limited palette of ink black, bone white,
blood red and a single gold accent; visible paper grain.

Composition: low horizon, big sky, strong diagonal from the ronin's shoulder to
the five shadows. Leave the upper-left and lower third relatively clean for a
title overlay.

Lighting: harsh backlight from a pale sun disc; long dramatic shadows; mist
between the figures.

Mood: stoic, disciplined, ominous, heroic — "the battle is internal".

No text in the image (title added separately). No gore, no modern items,
no photorealistic faces, no logos, no watermarks.
```

## Negative prompt

```
neon cyberpunk, 3d render, plastic gloss, chibi, cartoon mascot, modern city,
guns, gore, blood splatter, photorealistic face, text, watermark, logo,
extra limbs, blurry, low contrast
```

## Title overlay (add later)

- ID: `KENALI DIRIMU — 5 MUSUH`
- EN: `KNOW THYSELF — FIVE ENEMIES`

## Variasi

1. **Close-up** — hanya wajah ronin + 5 bayangan di matanya.
2. **Top-down** — ronin kecil di tengah, 5 bayangan mengelilingi seperti formasi.
3. **Minimalis** — siluet hitam di atas kertas putih, 5 goresan tinta merah.
